The problem

Too many hands between the farm and the market

Most of Nigeria's food is grown in the north, while many buyers are in the south and east. Today, reaching a farmer often means finding someone who knows someone who knows another person.

Today

Farmer→ Agent→ Middleman→ Middleman→ Wholesaler→ Retailer

Every extra hand adds a margin, a delay and a risk. The price rises before the food ever reaches the shelf.

With Agroflow

Farmer→ Wholesaler→ Retailer

Buyers order straight from farmers on one platform, with logistics, insurance and tracking built into every order.

How it works

From harvest to delivery in four steps

1

Farmers list produce

Farmers post what they grow, how much they have and when it will be ready.

2

Wholesalers order direct

Buyers anywhere in the country browse, compare and order straight from the source.

3

Insured logistics

Our logistics and insurance partners collect the goods and move them to the buyer with cover on the load.

4

Track and receive

Wholesalers watch the shipment on a live map, confirm delivery, then supply retailers.

Features

Built for trust across long distances

Buying from a farmer hundreds of kilometres away should feel as safe as buying down the road.

Direct marketplace

Farmers, wholesalers and retailers on one platform. No chains of introductions, no guesswork on who to call.

Insured transit

Goods travel with cover through our insurance partner, so a road incident doesn't wipe out a farmer's season or a buyer's order.

Live GPS tracking

Follow every shipment on a map in real time, with status updates from pickup to the buyer's door.

Fairer prices

With fewer middlemen taking a cut, more of the price reaches the farmer and less is added for the shopper.

Clear order records

Every order, delivery and payment is logged, giving buyers and farmers a trusted record to rely on.

Less waste

Faster routes from farm to market mean fresher produce and fewer losses along the way.
